The Aterean Empire was destroyed from within. At least, that’s how the story goes: the Age of Discovery saw rise to great cities with towering spires, academics branching their studies into known and unknown worlds, and the piecing together of dissociated knowledge to open terrifying new vistas of reality. Then the world went mad from their revelations.

It’s a distant memory now, here in the Age of Embers: the flames of the world are dying, and the land is crumbling to dust. Within the wretched halls of some far-flung prison for the unholy and condemned, the real story of the empire's downfall lies buried to time, in a repository found deep below ground, sealed by occult magic.

What are the chances that a prisoner might stumble into it?

—

In Chapter I: Prisoner, you play as the prisoner, eking out a meager existence amongst the condemned and the offal inside the foul halls of the Prisoners' Quarters. Propelled by history, the Prisoner works to dispel the magic sealing the Ancient Repository, taking them on a journey through the prison and out into the crumbling ruins of a once-great empire.

Explore the Prisoners' Quarters megastructure, make your way outside into the fractured and forgotten landscapes beyond, and uncover the truth of the empire's downfall in this thrilling opening to the Anthology trilogy. More information coming soon.
